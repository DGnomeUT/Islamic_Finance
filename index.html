    Islamic Finance & Structuring

    A comprehensive executive programme for senior practitioners — covering Shariah foundations, sukuk, private markets, governance, and cutting-edge innovation.

    Start Learning Reading List

    About This Course¶

    This programme is designed for senior executives, investment professionals, and lawyers who need a rigorous, practitioner-grade grounding in Islamic finance. It covers theory and practice — from first principles through to live deal structures, market data, and emerging innovations.

    The course is structured around 12 modules and a curated Essential Reading List, drawing on AAOIFI standards, IFSB reports, IIFM documentation frameworks, regulatory guidance, and primary deal documentation.

    Programme Structure¶

    Module 1

    Foundations of Islamic Finance — core philosophy, three prohibitions, positive structuring principles.

    Module 2

    Islamic Finance vs Conventional Finance — misconceptions, side-by-side comparison, substance vs form.

    Module 3

    Core Contracts & Structuring Toolkit — Murabaha, Ijara, Musharaka, Mudaraba, Sukuk, and more.

    Module 4

    Shariah Governance — multi-tier model, AAOIFI, IFSB, jurisdictional divergence, the fatwa process.

    Module 5

    Islamic Banking, Treasury & Liquidity — balance sheets, PSIA, liquidity instruments, treasury tools.

    Module 6

    Sukuk & Public Capital Markets — structure menu, asset-based vs asset-backed, ESG sukuk, screening.

    Module 7

    Islamic Finance in Private Markets — fund structuring, the halal waterfall, leverage, private credit.

    Module 8

    Case Study Compendium — UK Sovereign Sukuk, Dana Gas, Green Sukuk, PIF Programme, and more.

    Module 9

    Market Landscape & Ecosystem — USD 3.88–5.98 trillion market, three hubs, key players.

    Module 10

    Innovation & Debate — AAOIFI Standard 62, tokenisation, AI compliance, authenticity movement.

    Module 11

    Product Development & Opportunity Mapping — white space map, platform launch decision matrix.

    Module 12

    Executive Toolkit — five-question robustness test, red flags, due-diligence checklists.

    How to Use This Course¶

    Approach Guidance
    Sequential Work through Modules 1–12 in order for a complete grounding
    Reference Jump to any module using the left sidebar navigation
    Deal-ready Start with Module 3 (contracts), Module 6 (sukuk), and Module 12 (toolkit)
    Private markets Modules 7, 8, and 11 together form a private markets track

    Executive Refreshers

    Each module ends with a concise Executive Refresher — a set of key takeaways and mental models designed for rapid recall before client meetings or board discussions.

    Knowledge Checks

    Each module includes a Knowledge Check Quiz with model answers. Use these to test your understanding and identify gaps.
