    Essential Reading List¶

    This curated list covers the primary texts, regulatory standards, market reports, and critical analyses referenced throughout the course. Items are organised by category and annotated with their relevance and recommended depth of engagement.

    How to use this list

    • Core items are essential for all participants.
    • Practitioner Reference items should be on your desk — you will return to them repeatedly.
    • Annual Reference items should be read each year to stay current.
    • Case Study items support Module 8 directly.
    • Critical Perspective items challenge mainstream narratives — essential for balanced judgement.

    Foundational Texts¶

    Title Author / Publisher Relevance Level
    An Introduction to Islamic Finance: Theory and Practice Zamir Iqbal & Abbas Mirakhor / Wiley Finance (3rd ed.) Definitive academic foundation — Shariah principles, contract theory, banking and capital markets overview. Core
    Islamic Finance: Law, Economics, and Practice Mahmoud A. El-Gamal / Cambridge University Press Critical economic analysis of form vs substance — essential for understanding the mainstream academic critique. Core
    Principles of Islamic Finance Muhammad Taqi Usmani / IDARATUL MA'ARIF Authoritative scholarly perspective from one of the world's most prominent Shariah scholars. Core

    Standards and Regulatory Frameworks¶

    Title Author / Publisher Relevance Level
    AAOIFI Shariah Standards (complete set, 62+ standards) Accounting and Auditing Organisation for Islamic Financial Institutions / AAOIFI Primary reference for all contract structures — SS 8 (Murabaha), SS 9 (Ijara), SS 12 (Musharaka), SS 13 (Mudaraba), SS 17 (Sukuk), SS 59 (sale of debt), SS 62 (sukuk). Mandatory practitioner reference. Practitioner Reference
    IFSB Guiding Principles on Shariah Governance Systems (IFSB-10) Islamic Financial Services Board / IFSB Defines institutional Shariah governance framework — board composition, independence, competence, and reporting. Practitioner Reference
    IFSB Guiding Principles on Governance for Islamic Collective Investment Schemes (IFSB-6) Islamic Financial Services Board / IFSB Essential for fund structuring — governance of Mudaraba/Wakala-based collective investment vehicles. Practitioner Reference
    IFSB Islamic Financial Services Industry Stability Report (Annual) Islamic Financial Services Board / IFSB (annual publication) Best authoritative source for global market size data, structural trends, and systemic risk analysis. Annual Reference
    IIFM Sukuk Report and ISDA/IIFM Tahawwut Master Agreement International Islamic Financial Market / IIFM Primary documentation framework for sukuk and Islamic hedging. Critical for treasury and capital markets practitioners. Practitioner Reference

    Sukuk and Capital Markets¶

    Title Author / Publisher Relevance Level
    Sukuk Markets: A Practitioner's Guide Andreas A. Jobst & Juan Sole / IMF Working Paper Best concise practitioner-level treatment of sukuk structuring, asset-based vs asset-backed distinction, and regulatory implications. Core
    UK Government Sukuk Prospectus (2014) HM Treasury / UK Debt Management Office / UK DMO Primary document for the landmark first Western sovereign sukuk. Essential case study reference. Case Study
    IIFM Global Sukuk Market Report (Annual) International Islamic Financial Market / IIFM Authoritative annual data on global sukuk issuance volumes, structures, and market trends. Annual Reference
    Green Sukuk Guidance: A Framework for Sustainable Islamic Finance ICMA / IsDB / LSEG / ICMA Key framework for understanding green and sustainable sukuk convergence with ESG principles. Practitioner Reference

    Private Markets and Structured Finance¶

    Title Author / Publisher Relevance Level
    Islamic Finance in Private Equity and Venture Capital Various / DIFC / ADGM Publications / DIFC Practical treatment of fund structuring, halal waterfall mechanics, and leverage alternatives in Islamic PE. Core
    Islamic Private Credit: Emerging Strategies and Structures (PwC/DIFC, 2025) PwC / Dubai International Financial Centre / PwC / DIFC Most current practitioner analysis of commodity Murabaha-based drawdown facilities, digital onboarding, and evergreen credit strategies. Core
    AAOIFI Shariah Standard 3: Default in Payment AAOIFI / AAOIFI Critical reference for restructurings — defines penalty clause treatment and charity requirements for late payment. Practitioner Reference
    AAOIFI Shariah Standard 59: Sale of Debt AAOIFI / AAOIFI Governs constraints on distressed debt strategies and sukuk secondary market trading of receivable-heavy portfolios. Practitioner Reference

    Innovation, Digitalisation, and ESG¶

    Title Author / Publisher Relevance Level
    Tokenisation of Islamic Financial Instruments Securities Commission Malaysia / Khazanah / SC Malaysia Foundational regulatory framework for digital sukuk and tokenised Islamic instruments in Malaysia — the most advanced jurisdiction. Forward-Looking
    LSEG / ICD Islamic Finance Development Report (Annual) London Stock Exchange Group / ICD / LSEG / ICD Comprehensive annual market intelligence: USD 5.98 trillion market estimate, growth projections to USD 9.7 trillion by 2029. Annual Reference
    Malaysia SC Sustainable and Responsible Investment (SRI) Sukuk Framework Securities Commission Malaysia / SC Malaysia Regulatory framework enabling green, social, and sustainability-linked sukuk — template widely adopted regionally. Practitioner Reference

    Governance and Critical Analysis¶

    Title Author / Publisher Relevance Level
    Islamic Finance and the New Financial System Tariq Al-Rifai / Wiley Accessible executive-level overview of how Islamic finance intersects with global financial system architecture and ESG. Supplementary
    A Critique of Islamic Economics: The Case for Reform Timur Kuran / Princeton University Press Rigorous critical examination of the form vs substance debate and the political economy of Islamic finance. Essential for balanced perspective. Critical Perspective
    Dana Gas Sukuk Restructuring: Legal Analysis and Lessons Various (law firm client alerts: Allen & Overy, Clifford Chance, Linklaters) / Various law firm publications (2017-2018) Best practitioner analysis of the Dana Gas dispute — purchase undertaking enforceability, form vs substance risks, jurisdictional complexity. Case Study

    Indices and Market Data¶

    Title Author / Publisher Relevance Level
    MSCI Islamic Index Methodology MSCI / MSCI Definitive methodology for Shariah equity screening — business activity and financial ratio screens, buffer rules. Practitioner Reference
    S&P Global Shariah Indices Methodology S&P Dow Jones Indices / S&P Alternative major screening methodology — useful for comparing index construction and screened universe characteristics. Practitioner Reference

    How to Access These Resources¶

    Resource Type Access Route
    AAOIFI Standards Available via AAOIFI website (subscription) or through member institutions
    IFSB Publications Freely downloadable from ifsb.org
    IIFM Reports Freely available from iifm.net
    IMF Working Papers Freely available from imf.org
    Law firm client alerts Via firm websites or through Bloomberg Law/Practical Law
    SC Malaysia / BNM Freely available from sc.com.my and bnm.gov.my

    Note

    AAOIFI Shariah Standards are the single most important practitioner reference in this field. If you have access to only one resource, make it the complete AAOIFI Standards volume.
